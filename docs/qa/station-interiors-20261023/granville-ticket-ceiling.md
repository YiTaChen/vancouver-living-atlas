# Granville original ticket-hall ceiling

[Canmenwalker's actual 2026 concourse photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Granville_Station_Granville_Street_Concourse_2026.JPG)
(CC BY 4.0) shows a low ceiling of grey metal-mesh panels, pale rectangular
framing, and round stainless columns. It was inspected directly; the photograph
is not used as a game texture.

The original hall now has physical panel faces and projecting grid seams under
its existing ceiling shell. The 1.15 × 1 m module, 40 mm frames, frame depth and
panel finish are proportional interpretations, not surveyed dimensions or an
exact reproduction of the perforation pattern. Existing acoustic and cream
ceiling batches are reused. Floors, routes, stairs and boarding geometry are
unchanged; the strict 32-draw-call station limit remains in force.
