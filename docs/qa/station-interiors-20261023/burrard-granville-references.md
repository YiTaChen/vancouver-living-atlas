# Burrard and Granville: circulation and interior evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-23. The reconstruction preserves the game's existing platform
positions and heights. Street-to-hall placement, corridor lengths, bay widths,
stair dimensions and station depth are **representative game geometry**, not an
as-built survey. This evidence supports circulation relationships and visible
architectural character. It does not certify exact dimensions.

## Required reconstruction relationships

### Burrard

The glass ticket atrium is shared by the park/Burrard entrances and the mall
connections. The street approach has one up escalator next to a substantial
staircase. From the ticket hall, a bank of three escalators descends into the
enclosed underground circulation level. The two single-track platforms occupy
different heights. The lower outbound platform requires the additional short
vertical connector; the five-escalator count is one street escalator, three
main-bank escalators and one inter-platform escalator.

Stairs and all escalator banks must have continuous sidewalls, landing walls,
rails and physical soffits. The bright glass atrium should remain distinct from
the low, enclosed tunnel areas. Use the curved tunnel-wall and ceiling treatment
on both platform levels. Architectural roofs may fade for the game camera, while
collisions and enclosure remain present.

### Granville

Granville/Seymour entries reach the original ticket hall. The Bay corridor has a
short paired escalator connection. The ticket hall then uses the characteristic
three long parallel escalators in a single enclosed vaulted shaft. At its foot,
the circulation hub reaches the upper Waterfront-bound platform and the short
stair/escalator connection to the lower outbound platform.

The Dunsmuir/Hudson entrance is a separate circulation branch: its unpaid street
hall leads down three escalators to the tiled faregate hall beside the elevator.
The gate line belongs below the escalators, as visible in the actual photographs. It must not simply end
in a decorative lobby or be folded into the original long bank. A playable
accessible route must reach both stacked platforms.

The implemented Dunsmuir branch uses three parallel main escalators. This
grouping was verified by visually inspecting TransLink's December 2023 shaft
photograph, which is explicitly captioned as Granville's Dunsmuir exit. Its tall
rectangular tiled enclosure is distinct from the original Big 3's round acoustic
vault. A lower-platform stair and elevator connect this branch's platform halls;
the precise stair routing and internal turns remain representative. The original
bank's three parallel 35 m escalators, Bay pair and short inter-platform escalator
are separately evidenced.

## Acceptance observations and uncertainty

- Retain the existing 74 m track/platform alignments and train stop contracts.
- The upper and lower platforms must stay separate; there is no same-level
  cross-track island platform.
- Count visible escalators by their actual branch. Granville's six replaced
  original escalators and three Dunsmuir escalators must not be conflated.
- Verify the original entrance → ticketing/faregates → enclosed long descent →
  platform route, plus the short connector to the other platform. Dunsmuir uses
  the opposite faregate sequence: unpaid street hall → triple descent → gate line
  beside the elevator → platform access.
- Wall/soffit continuity and collision enclosure are acceptance requirements;
  roof transparency only changes rendering.
- Exact compass orientation of the subterranean branch junctions, every internal
  bend, all measured widths and actual floor elevations remain unverified here.
  They must not be advertised as survey-accurate.
- Contemporary elevator works at Burrard are temporary construction conditions;
  the reconstructed normal route models the permanent elevator connection.

## Physical verification

`node --test tests/dunsmuir-circulation.test.mjs` passes seven tests against the
actual `SkyTrainStationOwner` pedestrian runtime with `openWorld=false`:

- Every authored stair and all five Burrard / nine Granville escalators are
  traversed in both directions in 75 mm pedestrian steps.
- Each original entrance route passes the ticket hall and long bank, reaches the
  upper platform, then uses the short connector to the lower platform.
- Seymour's stair/passage, the unpaid Hudson Mall connection, the independent
  Dunsmuir descent and both Dunsmuir platform paths are continuously walkable.
- Both original 74 m platform polygons, platform/door contracts and every train
  anchor retain their existing coordinates.

The verification found and repaired underlying landing polygons that otherwise
trapped a reverse walker below visible stairs. Stair footprints are now cut from
both the support triangles and visible slabs. Bank walls follow the shaft slope
in short sections, preventing a deep shaft's bounding wall from blocking an
unrelated corridor above it. Corner passage walls have real door apertures.

Granville's three street entrances also retain the source entrance references'
relative offsets in the current runtime frame. This verifies the external door
relationships; it does not turn the representative underground geometry into a
measured station survey.
