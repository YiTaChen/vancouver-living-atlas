and Attribution License 1.0. It permits noncommercial academic research,
teaching, personal learning and attributed hobby demos; requires visible source
credit; and prohibits commercial exploitation without written permission,
plagiarism, credit removal and redistribution outside the granted terms.

The owner requested this change. Git history at the transition lists YiTaChen
as the project commit author; third-party components are expressly excluded.
This is custom source-available licensing, not OSI-approved open source, and has
not been reviewed by a lawyer. Legal review is advisable before relying on the
custom language for enforcement; publishing a license cannot technically prevent
copying or guarantee enforcement.

The preceding project revision is e380869. MIT-before-2026-09-06.txt preserves
the historical notice. Earlier MIT grants remain usable, including commercial
rights in previously released material. The change cannot retroactively take
those rights away, including when the same material remains in later revisions.
Future original additions distributed only under the new license do not receive
an MIT grant from this archive. Historical development reports and dated source
provenance may still describe the MIT license in force at their creation.

Data and dependency licenses are unchanged. The lockfile's root project license
changes; dependency package entries do not. Geographic source provenance is
retained. Do not treat this change as a relicensing of OSM/OGL data, fonts or
third-party software.
